Where the pull request does its real work: one or two sentences saying why this
waypoint is the core change of the branch. Optional, and absent on most
waypoints. It marks importance, not a defect; a defect is a finding.

Its presence is the flag. A waypoint that carries it draws a Core change chip
beside its title and a Core change note above its reading; a waypoint without it
draws neither. Write `null` to take one back.
