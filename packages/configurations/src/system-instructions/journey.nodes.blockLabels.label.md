The label of one argument, state, hook, effect, call or step of a node, drawn as a
row inside the node. It says what that block is for in the reader's terms:
what the value holds, or what the step does. A step is a statement with
all that is written inside it, or several statements that follow each
other, listed by the block of the first. Until it is written the row
shows the name of the code, such as `useApplyPromo`, marked as a draft.
