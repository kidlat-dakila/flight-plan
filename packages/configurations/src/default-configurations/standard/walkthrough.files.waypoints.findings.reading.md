### Reading

Explain the defect in the reader's file, one linked claim at a time: what the
code does, and why that is wrong. Every claim links the exact lines it rests on,
so the reader can open each one. Present tense, a few sentences at most.

Do not hedge — a reading that hedges is a reading nobody opened the file for.
Where you report a `risk` because you could not prove a suspected bug, the
reading says in one sentence what stopped you.

**An improvement says three things**: what the code does now and its limitation,
stated plainly as working; the better alternative, with the exact imported
symbol; and why it is better — fewer lines, fewer states, one fewer round trip.
Never "cleaner".
