### Solution

Every `bug`, `risk` and `nit` carries a solution. A `question` carries none: it
is not a claim.

Write the lines as they should be, whole, with the indentation they have in the
file. The solution replaces every line the finding's waypoint covers, so a line
you leave out of it is a line the fix deletes.

If the fix needs more than these lines, write the part that belongs to these
lines and name the other lines that must change in the reading, with `code:`
links. The waypoint says where the defect is, and the solution follows it.
