Say what this step does, and to what — "Throws when a line is out of stock", not "The stock check" and not the identifier. A bare noun tells a reader which code this is and nothing about why they are being shown it. The identifier is never the label: the node already carries the declaration's own name. Keep the same shape and tense on every node of one diagram.

An Entry Point Node has no code. Its label says what starts the flow from outside, in the reader's terms: "A customer submits the checkout form", "The nightly stock job runs".
