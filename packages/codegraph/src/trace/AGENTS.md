# packages/codegraph/src/trace

The band of a Trace Diagram: what the review page receives. Read [On the page](../../../../docs/vocabulary/trace-diagram.md#on-the-page) before changing anything here. The rules of [packages/codegraph/AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) hold here too.

## Rules

- **The band has no positions.** `buildTrace(segment, scope, places, context)` in `build.ts` turns the nodes of a segment's diagram into `TraceNodeView`s, nested as the nodes are, and the edges into their ids and their two ends. `packages/ui` places them.
- **A node view is words and code.** It carries the `type` and its label from `BLOCK_TYPE_LABELS`, the written label or the name of the code as a draft, the `name` of the code, the `shortPath` of the file, which is its last two folders and its name at most (`routes/orders/index.tsx`), the description as prose, and `change`: `added` when every line of the block the node stands for is new, `modified` when the node has another change, `null` when it has none. A top-level node an author has resized also carries its `width`, and a node that stores no width has no `width` at all.
- **A block is named in one place.** `nameOfBlock` in `names.ts` gives the name of a block, the name the catalogue gives it, or its type and its line. A call is named after the `binding` of its symbol when it has one, so a hook taken from a declaration of several names is named after the one imported. A block the catalogue says is a node of its own is named after the key it is written under, `list` of a tRPC router. An effect with no name is named after what it calls, `useMutation`. A `render-event` is named after its element and itself, `button.onClick`. A member read from an argument is named after the argument and itself, `props.onSave`. The band, the code links and the backend's node tools all read it.
- **Each card groups its rows as `cards/` says.** `listRowGroups` in `cards/index.ts` gives a node its `rowGroups`, each a label, the ids of its rows and the ids of the children drawn under it (`childIds`), which is the order the page draws them in. A function card, a component card and an effect or memo card take their groups from `function-card.ts`, `component-card.ts` and `hook-call-card.ts` instead, as the next rules say. `cards/component.ts`: `listComponentGroups`, the groups of a component card, Props, State, External calls (with `calleeCards`), Memos, Effects, Triggers, Renders (with `renderGroups`, its `rowIds` and `childIds` empty), and a group with no label that holds the children no render row holds, each left out when empty but External calls. `cards/class.ts`: the rows that are no property in the order they are written, then Properties, then Methods with the method children as `childIds`. `cards/function.ts`: any other node, such as an `effect`, a `component-effect` that is no effect hook (`useMutation`), a `resolver` or a `middleware`, one group with no label, its arguments first and every other row and trigger label in the order it is written. `cards/route.ts`: a route node lists no row, and puts each handler under the heading of its method, POST, PUT, PATCH, GET, DELETE, HEAD, OPTIONS, then a heading of several methods (`GET · POST`), then ANY, a heading that names a version (`GET v2`) after the heading of its method alone, headings of one rank in order (`compareHeadings`). `cards/router.ts`: a router lists no row, puts each procedure under Query, Mutation, Subscription or Field, and a child with no heading (a method of a NestJS resolver that resolves nothing) under Methods. `cards/store.ts`: a store puts its `part` rows and its children under the heading of their part, in the one order of `STORE_HEADINGS` every state library shares, and any other row after them with no label. `cards/headed-children.ts` puts the children of a node under the heading in their `card`. A child no group names, such as a class that extends another, is drawn after the groups. `cards/labels.ts` holds the labels of the kinds, and `labelOfHeading` turns a heading of a card, `EXTRA REDUCERS`, into the label of a group, `Extra reducers`. `blockLists` holds the rows by kind and is no order.
- **A function card is built in `function-card.ts`.** `isFunctionCardNode` in `build.ts` picks a node with no stored `rows`, that `listsNoRows` does not name, whose block `isFunctionCardBlock` (`trace-diagram/function-card.ts`). `buildFunctionCardView` reads its rows from `listFunctionCardRows` and gives its `blockLists` of the kinds `argument`, `call`, `return` and `throw`, and its `rowGroups`: Arguments, External calls, with one `TraceCalleeCardView` per callee in `calleeCards` (its id `<node id>:callee:<callee id>`, its name, its type and the type label the head of its own card reads (`typeLabelOfBlock` of `cards/index.ts`, through `headOfNode`: State for a store, API for an RTK Query API, Resolver for a procedure), Argument for a parameter, its `change` from `changeOfCallee`, Added when every line of the callee is new, and the ids of its call rows in the order written), Triggers, Returns and raises, and a group with no label that holds the children the node returns (`isReturned`). A group with nothing in it is left out (`isFilled`, which counts `calleeCards` and `renderGroups` as content), but External calls, whose heading the page leaves out when it holds no callee card, and the node has no `parameters`. `cardViewOf` in `build.ts` picks the builder of a function card, an effect or memo card or a component card, and a node with any of them has no `parameters` and takes its `blockLists` and `rowGroups` from it. An argument row is the parameter as written without its default (`parameterTextOf`), never a draft. A call row is named after the call as written, with `()`, a return row `return`, and a raise row the start of its statement (`statementStartOf`). A call or a return row has the id `siteIdOf` gives and the label of the `blockLabels` entry with the same block and `at`, and is changed when its line is (`changeOfSite`).
- **A component card is built in `component-card.ts`.** `isComponentCardNode` in `build.ts` picks a node with no stored `rows`, that `listsNoRows` does not name, whose block `isComponentCardBlock` (`trace-diagram/component-card.ts`, a `component`). `buildComponentCardView` reads its rows from `listComponentCardRows` and gives its `blockLists` of the kinds `argument` (its props), `state`, `call`, `memo`, `effect` and `render`. A prop row is the prop with its type as `propTextOf` of `component-code.ts` gives it, but an emitted event, and a state row the name and its starting value as `stateTextOf` gives it, both never a draft, with the id of their block. A call row is the call row of a function card. A memo row and an effect row have the id of their block and the label of the `blockLabels` entry of that block with no `at`, and are named after the call: `teamTypes = useMemo()` (`memoCallOf` of `trace-diagram/hook-calls.ts`), `useEffect()`. A render row is the label row of a return group, with the id `siteIdOf` gives the block around the `return` and its `at`, or the `render-root` of a body, or a render row of a group, with the id of its block, and of its block and `at` for a value row. Each takes the label of the `blockLabels` entry of the same block and `at`. A prop, a state, a memo, an effect and a render row are changed when a block inside their block is (`changeWithin`), and the lines of a return group run from the `return` to the last line of its markup. Its Renders group holds one `TraceRenderGroupView` per return group: the id `<node id>:render:<site id>`, the name `renderGroupNameOf` gives, the `rowId` of its label row, and its `rows`, each with its `rowId`, its `kind` and the `childIds` of the child drawn inside it. A child goes in the first component row or value row that renders it (`rowIdByChildIdOf`), and a row whose component is no child of the node holds none, and a child no row holds is in the group with no label after the others.
- **`component-code.ts` writes the code of a component's rows.** `propTextOf` gives a prop as written when it holds its type, or else its name and the annotation found after it on its line, in the props type written in the head of the component (`annotationInHead`), or in an interface or a type alias of the same file the component names as a type (`annotationInDeclarations`), the `?` of an optional prop kept, or its name alone. `isEmittedEvent` says a prop is an event `defineEmits` declares, and `buildComponentCardView` lists no row for it. `stateTextOf` gives `name = value` from the declaration as written: a `useState` keeps its first name and its argument in place of the call, and any other declaration that takes names apart keeps its pattern as written (`assignedTargetOf` of `parameters.ts`), `[bag, addItem, clear] = useUnit([…])`. `renderGroupNameOf` gives `return` and the start of what it returns, the first element as `<tag>` or the first word (`return null`), `<template>` for the template of a Vue file, and `=>` with the first element for the body of an arrow function. `renderRowNameOf` gives a condition as `{… && <p>}`, `{… ? <p> : …}` or `<p v-if>`, a loop as the call that maps, `trainer.team.map()`, or `<li v-for>`, a component row as the name of the loop around it, of the condition around it with its own element, the branch of a ternary it is in, `{… ? <Empty> : …}` or `{… ? … : <Team>}`, and `<Team v-else>` for a Vue chain, or `<TeamSlot />` when none is around it, and a value row as `{badges}` or `{renderBadges()}`.
- **An effect or a memo card is built in `hook-call-card.ts`.** `isHookCallCardNode` in `build.ts` picks a node with no stored `rows`, that `listsNoRows` does not name, whose block `isHookCallBlock` (`trace-diagram/hook-calls.ts`). `buildHookCallCardView` reads its rows from `listHookCallCardRows` and gives its `blockLists` of the kinds `dependency`, `outside-variable`, `call`, `return` and `throw`, and its `rowGroups`: Dependencies, Outside variables, External calls with its `calleeCards`, Triggers, Returns and raises, each left out when empty but External calls. A dependency row is the name as written where it is used, never a draft. An outside variable row is the name of what it reads, a state setter as written, with what it is as its label, from `OUTSIDE_VARIABLE_LABELS` (Constant, State, State setter, Prop, Variable, Function, Import), never a draft and never changed. A dependency and an outside variable row have the id `siteIdOf` gives their block and the place of the name. Calls, returns and raises are the rows of a function card.
- **`row-names.ts` names the rows an agent writes a label for, as the band draws them.** `listCardRowNames(node, sources)`, exported from the entry, gives the name and the kind (`CardRowKind`) of each such row of a function card, an effect or memo card or a component card, keyed by the id `siteIdOf` gives: a call as `callNameOf`, a return or a raise as `exitNameOf` (both of `function-card.ts`, handed the `SourceFile` of the block or `null`), a memo or an effect as `hookCallNameOf` of `component-card.ts`, a return group as `renderGroupNameOf` and a render row as `renderRowNameOf` with its render kind. It picks the card with `isFunctionCardNode`, `isComponentCardNode` and `isHookCallCardNode` of `build.ts`, and any other node has none. `cardRowNameOf(label, rowNames)` gives the name of one `blockLabels` entry. The backend's MCP tools read them, so an agent sees the names the page draws.
- **`destructured-units.ts` sends a name taken apart from a list to its unit.** `withDestructuredUnits`, applied to the calls of a function card, an effect or memo card and a component card, reads a call of a name declared by an array pattern from a call of a store, `const [bag, addItem] = useUnit([$bag, itemAdded])`, finds the unit at the same place in the list the declaration reads (`patternNamesOf`, `unitsOf`), and gives the call that unit as its `called` block, and as its callee when the unit has a store or API card of its own; the store stays the callee otherwise.
- **Some cards head differently.** `headOfNode` in `cards/index.ts` gives the name and the type label of a node's head: a route node is named after its path and reads Route, a router reads Router, a router whose node is a class (a NestJS resolver) reads Resolver, a class with a `controller` card reads Controller, an RTK Query API reads API, a server action (a `route` card of the part `server-action`) reads Server action, a memo (`isMemoBlock` of `trace-diagram/hook-calls.ts`) reads Memo, and every other node is named after its block and reads the label of its type. `listsNoRows` says which nodes list no row at all: a route and a router.
- **A trigger of an event handed a function has the lines of that function too.** `linesWithHandlers` adds them, `hasChange` marks the trigger when the function changed, and `codeLinesOf` gives the node of the event the lines of the function as its code.
- **A node lists its arguments, its state, its hooks, its effects, its calls and its steps.** `toBlockLists` in `build.ts` reads the listed blocks of a node from `listListedBlocks` of `trace-diagram/listed-blocks.ts`, gives each the label of its `blockLabels` entry, or the name of its code as a draft, and gives one `blockLists` entry per kind that has a block, in the order `argument`, `property`, `state`, `hook`, `effect`, `call`, `step`, `part`, for a node that is no function card, no component card and no effect or memo card. A hook is an `external-value` with a call of a name that starts with `use` and a capital letter, listed under that name. An effect inside a `state` or a `derived` declaration of the node is listed as that declaration, under `state`, whether or not a chain passes through the declaration. A step is any other block that `isStepBlock` names, that is a node of its own or that has a change, but an event, a data fetch, markup and a function written in place of an event or a callback. What is inside a listed block is folded into its row, and the statements that follow each other are one step, as [Steps](../../../../docs/vocabulary/trace-diagram.md#steps) says.
- **A row carries the lines of its block.** Each listed block and each trigger label has `lines`, the line spans of its block, so the page can focus the code of the node on them. A step of several statements has one span, from the first line of the first to the last line of the last.
- **A row is changed when its block is, or the function written in place of it, or a block folded into it.** `changeOfBlock` marks an event whose arrow function changed, and `toBlockLists` marks the row `rowIdAround` gives each changed block of the node.
- **A line leaves the row of the block that takes the step.** `rowFinderOf` gives each node a `RowFinder`. Both card finders are a `siteRowFinder`, handed the calls of the node, its argument or prop blocks (`parameters`), the row ids a line leaves (`fromRowIds`) and the row ids a line reaches (`toRowIds`), each found from the block or the nearest block around it. A step from a block that names the reached node inside an argument, in its type, its default or a `Depends(…)` (`argumentNaming`), leaves that argument's row, and a step no call row takes leaves the argument whose blocks reach the node (`parameterReaching`), or the head. A call row takes a step only when its own call reaches the block: its `called` block, or its callee when it calls the callee itself or a block inside it (`isCallingCallee`), and a call of a library also when its block hands over a value that reaches it (`handsOver`). `calledOf` gives the called block of a call row, so a step that reaches the head of a node whose part the call names, an action of a store, reaches the row of that part instead (`calledInside` in `build.ts`). For a function card and an effect or memo card it is `functionCardRowFinder`, with its argument rows as rows a line leaves: `fromRowOf` gives the call row whose called block is the block reached, or whose callee is it or holds it, looked for among the calls of the stepping block and the blocks around it first, then among every call of the node; `toRowOf` gives an argument or a raise row, or the head. For a component card it is `componentRowFinder`: a line leaves a prop row, the row of a memo, an effect or a trigger label from inside its block, the call row of a node the component holds that is a call, `api.todo.create.useMutation({ onSuccess })`, the component row of the `render` block that renders a child, or else a call row as in a function card, and reaches the head. For any other node, `rowIdByBlockIdOf` gives every statement of a step the id of its row, and `rowIdAround` the row of a block or the nearest one around it. `toEdgeViews` gives an edge one view per step between its two nodes (`listNodeSteps` of `trace-diagram/node-blocks.ts`), and one from the row of an inner node's block to the head of that node. `withoutVaguerViews` drops a view that reaches the head of a node when another from the same row reaches one of its rows, so a call draws one line, to the most specific row. An edge with no such view, one a card rule draws, such as the line from a hook of an RTK Query API to its endpoint, leaves the row `rowCalling` gives: the call row whose called block is the node block reached, or whose callee is it or holds it. A step that leaves the block of a node, or reaches it, has no row on that side.
- **A node shows the code of its block.** `buildNodeCodeViews` in `code-views.ts` gives a node one view of the lines of the block it stands for, and of every folded value it holds (`codeLinesOf`), read from the source as the Code Walkthrough reads it, with a `waypoint` row above the first line each waypoint covers (above the first line shown when the waypoint starts earlier) and a note for a file outside the segment's `changedPaths`. `listWaypointPlaces` in `walkthrough/build.ts` gives the visible waypoints with their ranges, and the caller hands them to `buildTrace`.
