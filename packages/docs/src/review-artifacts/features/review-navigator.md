The Review Navigator is the toolbar at the foot of the review page. It holds the controls of the section on your screen, and every control has a key, so you can read a whole review without leaving the keyboard.

![Jumping to the Blast Diagram from the Review Navigator, then on to the Code Walkthrough and its next waypoints. The toolbar changes its controls with each section](../../../media/review-navigator-light.webm)

## On every section

| Control | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Jump to** | W | Lists the sections of the segment on screen, then the other segments, and jumps to the one you pick. |
| **Back** | A | Goes to the section before. |
| **Next** | D | Goes to the section after, and on into the next segment. |

## The controls of each section

| Section | Controls |
|---|---|
| Context | **Expand all** or **Collapse all** (Q), when the Context has sections that fold |
| Trace Diagram | **Collapse all** or **Expand all** cards (Q), **Full screen** (F), and **Explain this card** (G) while a card is open. On your own Flight Plan, **Add card** (C), **Clear diagram** (K) and **Remove diagram** (L), which ask you first before they clear or remove anything |
| Blast Diagram | **Export bound** (E) and **Import bound** (Q), **Full screen** (F), and **Assess this file** (G) while a file is open |
| Code Walkthrough | **Previous waypoint** (A), the position, **Next waypoint** (D), **Mark as reviewed** (S), **Add comment** (C), **Let Claude explain** (G), **Explain the whole code block** (Ctrl+G), **Filter** (F), **Collapse all** or **Expand all** files (Q), **Collapse file** or **Expand file** (E), **Expand waypoint** or **Collapse waypoint** (R) |

In the Code Walkthrough, **Previous waypoint** and **Next waypoint** take the place of Back and Next. They step through the waypoints of the segment, and at either end carry on to the section before or after. On your own Flight Plan, **Write description** (C) takes the place of Add comment, and there is no Mark as reviewed.

Zoom in and Zoom out are buttons at the top right of a diagram, beside a **Full screen** button that does what F does.

## When the keys do nothing

The keys do nothing while you type in a field, such as a comment or the chat, while a dialog is open, or while Cmd or Alt is held. With Ctrl held, only Ctrl+G does something, and every other key is left to the browser.
