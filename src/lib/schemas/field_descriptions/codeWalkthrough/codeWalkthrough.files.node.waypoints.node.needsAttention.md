Why this waypoint is one of the few a reviewer should focus on: a string of one
or two sentences saying where the pull request does its real work. Optional, and
absent on most waypoints. It marks importance, not a defect; a defect is a
finding.

Its presence is the flag. A waypoint that carries it draws a Needs Attention
chip beside its title and a Needs Attention section above its reading; a
waypoint without it draws neither, so there is no way to have a chip with no
reasoning behind it.
